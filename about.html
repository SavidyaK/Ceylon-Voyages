Founded in 2012

Born from a Passion for Island Exploration

Ceylon Voyages began over a decade ago when two certified tour chauffeurs joined forces to offer travelers something deeper than crowded tour buses: authentic, private, and flexible journeys.

Over the years, we have grown into a premier destination management agency based in Colombo, having guided more than 15,000 visitors from the United Kingdom, Australia, Europe, and the Americas.

Every chauffeur in our fleet is vetted, background-checked, and licensed by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA). We treat our guests as personal friends, sharing local delicacies, tucked-away temple viewpoints, and secluded beaches you won't find in ordinary guidebooks.
